# MERCHBOY DIGITAL: OPERATION 200X (THE SINGULARITY) 🧠⚡⚡
> "We need to go deeper." - The Architect

This is the expansion pack. Ideas 101-200.

## SECTION 11: THE UNDERGROUND (DARK WEB ECONOMY) 🕵️‍♂️
101. **Black Market**: A hidden shop that only opens at random times with "illegal" glitched items.
102. **Data Smuggling**: A runner minigame where you transport packets while dodging firewalls.
103. **Cipher Key**: An item that decrypts other users' "Private" status messages.
104. **Virus Bomb**: Temporarily scrambles a friend's screen (for fun/prank).
105. **Cleaner Service**: Pay coins to remove negative effects/viruses instantly.
106. **Anonymous Mode**: Buy a mask to appear as "Unknown User" on leaderboards.
107. **Bribe**: Pay the system to re-roll your Daily Missions.
108. **Counterfeit Merch**: Create "fake" rarity items (that might dissolve after 24h).
109. **Signal Jammer**: Prevents others from viewing your profile for 1 hour.
110. **The Vault**: A community pot where stolen coins go; guess the password to win it all.

## SECTION 12: GUILDS & FACTIONS (TRIBALISM) ⚔️
111. **Corporate Sponsorship**: Join "Team Neon" or "Team Void" for exclusive skins.
112. **Guild Halls**: A shared visual lobby for clan members.
113. **Turf Wars**: Guilds compete for control of specific Arcade games (visual branding takes over).
114. **Tax System**: Guild leaders can tax member earnings for the guild bank.
115. **Collaborative Projects**: Determine the next app update by Guild vote weight.
116. **Uniforms**: Auto-equip a specific color palette for all members.
117. **Mentorship**: High-level players get bonuses for partying with low-level players.
118. **Guild Raids**: Multi-user boss fights (clicker style) with massive HP.
119. **Banners**: Custom pixel art flags displayed on member profiles.
120. **Treachery**: A mechanic to spy on other guilds' chat.

## SECTION 13: ARCADE PHASE 2 (NEXT GEN) 🕹️
121. **Dungeon Crawler**: A procedurally generated roguelike using ASCII art.
122. **Beat 'Em Up**: Side-scrolling brawler using your Avatar.
123. **Puzzle Fighter**: Match-3 game that sends "junk blocks" to your opponent.
124. **Fishing Derby**: Live 10-player fishing tournament (biggest fish wins).
125. **Tamagotchi Arena**: Auto-battler using your Pocket Bro stats.
126. **Typing of the Dead**: Kill zombies by typing fast (good for coders).
127. **Paintball**: Top-down shooter aimed at coloring the map your team's color.
128. **Casino Night**: Poker/Blackjack using in-game coins (high stakes).
129. **Speedrun Mode**: Global timer for "All Games" completion.
130. **Level Editor**: Users design platformer levels for others to play.

132. **Vehicle Garage**: Park your "Merch Kart" or "Space Ship" on your profile.

## SECTION 15: THE MERCHVERSE (IMMERSIVE SPACES) 🌐
141. **The Cinema**: Watch YouTube videos together in a virtual theater.
142. **The Club**: Visualizer room where the lights sync to the music.
